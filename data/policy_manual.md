# County Benefits Policy Manual (Synthetic, Q3 2026)

## Clause 1.1 — Eligibility for General Assistance
An applicant is eligible for General Assistance if their household gross monthly income is at or below 130% of the federal poverty line for their household size, and they are a resident of the county for at least 30 consecutive days prior to application.

## Clause 1.2 — Eligibility for Emergency Food Assistance
Emergency Food Assistance does not require proof of county residency. Applicants must self-attest to household income at or below 185% of the federal poverty line. No waiting period applies.

## Clause 2.1 — Required Documentation
Applicants must provide one proof of identity (driver's license, state ID, or passport) and one proof of address (utility bill or lease dated within the last 60 days). Proof of income is required only for General Assistance, not for Emergency Food Assistance.

## Clause 2.2 — Missing Documentation
If an applicant cannot provide proof of address, caseworkers may accept a signed self-attestation form (Form B-12) in its place, valid for 90 days, after which standard documentation is required.

## Clause 3.1 — Processing Timelines
General Assistance applications must be processed within 10 business days of a complete application being received. Emergency Food Assistance applications must be processed within 2 business days.

## Clause 3.2 — Incomplete Applications
An incomplete application pauses the processing clock. The applicant has 30 calendar days to submit missing items before the application is administratively closed.

## Clause 4.1 — Appeals Process
An applicant denied benefits may request a fair hearing within 60 calendar days of the denial notice. Hearings are scheduled within 15 business days of the request.

## Clause 4.2 — Benefit Reduction Notices
Any reduction in existing benefits requires a written notice at least 10 calendar days before the reduction takes effect, except in cases of confirmed fraud.

## Clause 5.1 — Recertification
General Assistance recipients must recertify eligibility every 6 months. Emergency Food Assistance recipients must recertify every 12 months.

## Clause 5.2 — Change in Circumstances
Recipients must report any change in household income or size within 10 calendar days. Failure to report may result in overpayment recovery under Clause 6.1.

## Clause 6.1 — Overpayment Recovery
Overpayments caused by unreported changes are recovered by withholding up to 10% of future monthly benefits until repaid, unless the recipient requests a hardship waiver.

## Clause 7.1 — Interpreter and Accessibility Services
Applicants may request a free interpreter for any language, and accommodations for disabilities, at any point in the application or appeals process. Requests may not delay processing timelines under Clause 3.1.
